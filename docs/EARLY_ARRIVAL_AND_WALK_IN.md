# Early attendance and walk-in entry
Booking QR issuance and scanning now allow arrival any time after booking creation, through the saved grace deadline. The original appointment and cancellation deadline remain unchanged; attendance session duration begins when gym staff scan the QR. Cancellation, no-show allocation, session validation and single-settlement guards are retained.
The trainee QR tab and home shortcut show the normal walk-in QR again. No prior reservation is required. Bookings remain reachable from the QR page; branch reservation confirmation opens booking history directly. Booking QR payloads keep the gymbooking: prefix and must use the booking scan action; ordinary QR uses the existing walk-in attendance flow.
Database integration coverage confirms a booking one hour in the future can be scanned now, without changing its appointment or charging a second time. Web and native window tests include early arrival. No schema or production database changes are required.
