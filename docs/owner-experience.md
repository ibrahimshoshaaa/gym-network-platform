# Owner mobile workspace

The owner navigation is Home, Visits, Scan QR, Finance and More. More groups branch management, services/facilities, team, ads, maintenance, marketplace, account/security/language, notifications and support. With one branch, Services and Team open directly; multiple branches use a branch picker. Scan QR opens the existing confirmation flow directly for one operational branch and preserves branch selection for multi-branch owners. Permanent branch QR remains available.

Home shows net available funds, daily visits and net visit revenue, a seven-day daily visit chart, recent visits and direct work shortcuts. Date defaults come from the API's Cairo calendar. Figures are scoped server-side and report errors do not fabricate balances. The chart excludes cancelled visits. Net visit receivables retain the existing report accounting definition.

Visits/reports offer today, seven days, thirty days, custom periods up to one year, branch selection and paginated records. Finance groups overview, withdrawal history and reports; existing payout approvals, debts and confirmation safeguards remain in place. Branch services has separate Services, Facilities and Hours tabs. Picking a service collapses the preset list to keep price/duration fields close at hand.

Booking readiness: explicit service hours may be used without general branch hours. Any selected service inheriting branch hours still requires configured branch hours. When general branch hours exist they also bound the visit. The full combined session must fit all applicable windows. Snapshotted windows limit later attendance as well; changes cannot silently alter an existing booking's terms. No schema migration or financial policy change is required.
